# Competitor Brief

Дата: 05.06.2026.

## Nearest Substitutes

- Musician portfolio builders such as Stagefolio and Bandzoogle-like flows emphasize biography, albums, video/gallery, contacts and streaming consolidation.
- Independent label sites such as Fellas Music Label, McCandless Records and Brave Wave tend to organize around roster, releases and label identity.
- Creative archive portfolios such as Tape Lab combine discography with broader visual/art/project framing.

## What The Niche Already Does

- Uses a hero brand statement plus a grid of albums or artists.
- Pushes visitors toward Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube.
- Often relies on real cover art as the primary visual substance.
- For labels, clarity of roster and genre separation matters more than long manifesto copy.

## Risks To Avoid

- Fake streaming links before actual URLs exist.
- One visual tone for all artists, which would erase the difference between pop, classical, Indian / Asia and Chinese pop lines.
- Generic dark music-site template with a large headline and anonymous cards.
- Overpromising “label scale” or “millions of streams” without source data.

## Chosen Advantage

The site owns a catalog-map identity: it treats the project as an evolving AI music label / producer archive with clear artist lanes, release chronology and tracklists. That is more honest and useful than a glossy landing page without real DSP links.
