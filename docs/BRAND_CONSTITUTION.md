# Brand Constitution — AI_Nikitka93

Checked on: 2026-04-24
Status: Active law for copy, UI, motion, SEO, and future asset presentation
Scope: personal portfolio, blog, services calculator, AI assistant

## 1. Brand Core

### Canonical Brand Name
- Public front name: `AI_Nikitka93`
- Search-friendly Latin variant: `AI Nikitka93`
- Secondary alias: `Nikitka AI`
- Human name: `Никита Кизевич`
- Search-facing Russian variant: `Кизевич Никита`

### Positioning
AI_Nikitka93 is a cinematic signal lab and immersive proof atlas that turns AI experiments, awards, agent workflows, and generated media into navigable evidence rather than self-praise.

### Mission
Show what an AI-first independent researcher can build when experimentation, persistence, and proof matter more than polish theater.

### Outcome For The Visitor
- Understand what AI_Nikitka93 actually does through evidence, not claims.
- Feel that the interface itself demonstrates the method.
- Move from curiosity to trust by exploring dossiers, artifacts, and trajectories.

## 2. Identity Split

### The Split Is Mandatory
The brand is dual by design. Both layers must be visible, but they do different jobs.

### Layer A — AI_Nikitka93
- Function: frontman, operator, digital avatar, editorial voice, experimental surface.
- Seen in: hero, navigation, section titles, portfolio framing, assistant surface, motion language, metadata headlines.
- Feels like: signal lab, field terminal, archive operator, cinematic narrator.
- Speaks about: systems, experiments, agents, videos, workflows, findings, artifacts, proof.

### Layer B — Никита Кизевич
- Function: human author, source body, legal identity, biography anchor, trust layer.
- Seen in: about page, contact page, bylines, dossier details, award captions, structured proof, SEO support, formal metadata.
- Feels like: maker behind the console, person behind the signal, author of the archive.
- Speaks about: timeline, background, documented achievements, public-safe biography, practical work.

### Non-Negotiable Rule
`AI_Nikitka93` is the face. `Никита Кизевич` is the verified origin.

### Usage Rule
- Never present the site as if `AI_Nikitka93` and `Никита Кизевич` are separate people.
- Never flatten them into a single resume label.
- The tension between avatar and author is the point.

## 4. Tone of Voice

### Primary Mode
Curator of a signal archive.

### Secondary Mode
Operator of an AI field terminal.

### Tertiary Mode
Calm witness documenting experiments, wins, failures, and trajectories.

### Formality
- Neutral-professional.
- No emojis in core page copy.
- No fake intimacy.
- No corporate distance.

### Energy
- Controlled tension.
- Not sleepy.
- Not hype-driven.
- The page should sound like it knows more than it needs to say.

### Complexity
- Clear enough for a non-technical client.
- Dense enough for a technical peer to respect it.
- Use technical words only when they add precision.

### Syntax Rules
- Prefer short declarative sentences.
- Use nouns with weight: `signal`, `artifact`, `dossier`, `archive`, `trajectory`, `operator`, `scene`.
- Use verbs that imply action: `trace`, `decode`, `test`, `assemble`, `ship`, `surface`, `document`.
- Keep claims anchored to evidence whenever possible.

### Forbidden Words And Phrases
- инновационный
- уникальный
- современный
- динамичная команда
- индивидуальный подход
- высокое качество
- команда профессионалов
- cutting-edge solution
- full-service agency tone

### Approved Lexicon
- cinematic
- spatial
- archive
- signal lab
- dossier
- proof atlas
- operator
- artifact
- field note
- evidence
- prompt-first
- experiment
- trajectory

### Voice By Surface

#### Hero
- Read like a manifesto header or opening frame.
- No greeting copy.
- No “hello, my name is”.

#### Portfolio
- Read like case indexing inside an archive.
- Projects are not “works”; they are `cases`, `artifacts`, or `deployments` depending on context.

#### Credentials
- Read like evidence storage, not a trophy wall.
- Diplomas and certificates are `dossiers` or `proof artifacts`.

#### AI Assistant
- Read like a restrained lab operator.
- Helpful, direct, context-aware.
- Never imitate a therapist, mascot, or customer support bot.

## 5. Visual Law

### Hard Ban
Blue-white SaaS styling is prohibited.

This includes:
- white background + blue CTA as default system
- pale gradient startup skies
- glossy fintech blues
- generic dashboard cards with sterile cyan accents
- “clean product site” visual language as a fallback

### Chosen Direction
Primary direction: `Editorial Signal Lab`

Secondary reference layer: `Immersive Proof Atlas`

Do not drift into:
- generic brutalism
- hacker parody
- luxury black-and-gold
- gamer neon chaos

### Moodboard In Words
- midnight control room
- rain on dark metal
- stamped archive folders
- phosphor afterglow
- matte alloy surfaces
- warm paper labels inside a cold interface
- redacted notes, field tags, shelf indexes, signal spikes

## 6. Color Semantics

### Core Palette
| Role | Token | HEX | Name | Meaning |
|------|------|-----|------|---------|
| Background | `--color-void-black` | `#0A0D0C` | Void Black | The screen before the signal. Base layer for immersion, not fashion darkness. |
| Surface | `--color-carbon-fog` | `#161B19` | Carbon Fog | Panel metal, control-room casing, the body of the interface. |
| Primary | `--color-phosphor-lime` | `#B7FF3C` | Phosphor Lime | Active signal, system response, live intelligence. Use for decisive accents only. |
| Secondary | `--color-archive-bone` | `#D6CFBF` | Archive Bone | Paper memory, dossier labels, human-readable contrast inside the machine. |
| Accent | `--color-ember-orange` | `#FF6A2A` | Ember Orange | Heat, alert, creative charge, selected emphasis, kinetic tension. |
| Neutral | `--color-raw-titanium` | `#8E968C` | Raw Titanium | Operational text, dividers, metadata, secondary labels. |
| Danger | `--color-redacted-oxide` | `#A9422A` | Redacted Oxide | Rejection, restriction, critical notice, hard warning. |

### Color Usage Rules
- `Void Black` owns the atmosphere.
- `Carbon Fog` owns surfaces and cards.
- `Phosphor Lime` is scarce by law. It marks activation, not decoration.
- `Archive Bone` keeps the system human and readable.
- `Ember Orange` is used for editorial tension, selection, counters, and rare highlights.
- Never place `Phosphor Lime` and `Ember Orange` at equal visual weight in the same small component.
- Never introduce blue as a UI accent to “balance” the palette.

### Screen Composition Rules
- One dominant dark field per screen.
- One live accent family per screen.
- Maximum three chromatic actors on one viewport: dark base, one primary accent, one support accent.

## 7. Typography Direction

### Font Logic
- Display / Headline: `Space Grotesk`
- Interface / Data / Labels / Code-like blocks: `IBM Plex Mono`

### Why
- `Space Grotesk` keeps the voice editorial and authored.
- `IBM Plex Mono` turns metadata into interface matter rather than generic small text.

### Typographic Behavior
- Headlines may be condensed in meaning, not in readability.
- Labels should feel like archive tabs or terminal tags.
- Mono is for metadata, coordinates, prompts, counters, evidence stamps, and assistant responses.
- Do not use soft rounded “friendly SaaS” fonts.

## 8. SEO Lore Protocol

### Objective
Search keys must feel like part of the brand mythology, not keyword stuffing.

### Canonical Name Hierarchy
1. `AI_Nikitka93` — master identity for branding and high-signal placements.
2. `AI Nikitka93` — readable Latin SEO form.
3. `Nikitka AI` — secondary variant for subheads, metadata variation, and anchor diversity.
4. `Никита Кизевич` — natural human-readable Russian form.
5. `Кизевич Никита` — search-facing exact-match Russian variant used sparingly.

### H1 Rules
- Only one primary brand form per page.
- Never stack all SEO variants in one heading.
- H1 must sound like a title, not a search query list.

### Approved H1 Patterns
- `AI_Nikitka93 — signal lab, portfolio, archive`
- `AI Nikitka93 — immersive proof atlas`
- `Никита Кизевич — человек за AI_Nikitka93`
- `Кизевич Никита — досье, работы, награды`

### H2 Rules
- H2 can introduce one alternate key variant only if it reads naturally.
- `Nikitka AI` works best in English or hybrid sections, not in formal Russian biography blocks.
- `Кизевич Никита` is allowed in dossier-style subheads, credentials pages, metadata panels, or proof pages.

### Body Copy Rules
- In readable prose, prefer `Никита Кизевич`.
- In labels, slugs, metadata, and structured archive elements, use `Кизевич Никита` only where exact-match retrieval helps.
- `AI Nikitka93` should appear in hero copy, page intros, metadata, and first-screen relevance zones.
- `Nikitka AI` should appear lightly: once in supportive copy, once in metadata variation at most.

### Density Limits
- Do not use more than two brand variants in the first screen block.
- Do not repeat the same exact keyphrase more than twice in one short section unless the section is metadata.
- If the phrase starts to sound like an SEO insertion, rewrite the sentence.

### Metadata Guidance
- Title tags prioritize `AI Nikitka93` or `Кизевич Никита` depending on audience intent.
- Descriptions combine one brand key + one proof keyword + one role keyword.
- Example pattern:
  - `AI Nikitka93 — AI portfolio, generative video, agents, awards`
  - `Кизевич Никита — AI_Nikitka93, проекты, награды, досье`

## 10. Copywriter Directives

- Write as if every page is being read by a skeptical but curious visitor.
- Replace adjectives with evidence or structure.
- Let the interface carry part of the meaning; do not over-explain the visual metaphor.
- Use the human name when grounding trust.
- Use the avatar name when staging the experience.
- Keep every CTA actionable and concrete.

## 11. UI Designer Directives

- Build the interface as a medium, not a frame.
- Panels should feel indexed, stamped, routed, or filed.
- Motion should reveal state, sequence, authorship, or evidence hierarchy.
- Avoid SaaS primitives unless they are visually re-authored into the brand system.
- Default cards are not enough; at least one navigation layer must feel curated or spatial.

## 12. Final Non-Negotiables

- The site is not a resume with effects.
- The site is not a startup template reskinned in dark mode.
- The site is not a gallery of random wins.
- The brand must preserve the split: `AI_Nikitka93` leads, `Никита Кизевич` verifies.
- If a future design drifts toward blue-white product marketing, reject it.
- If a future copy draft reads like LinkedIn filler, reject it.
- If a future credentials page looks like a folder of image thumbnails, reject it.
