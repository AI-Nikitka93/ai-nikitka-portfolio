# AI_Nikitka93 Portfolio Design System

## Identity & Tone
- **Identity:** AI_Nikitka93 (Lead Persona / Verified Author)
- **Concept:** Auteur AI portfolio, framed as a film production or cinematic archive.
- **Languages:** Russian (Default), English (Secondary via header switch). All text localized.

## Visual Style
- **Theme:** Cinematic Dark Mode.
- **Composition:** Sparse, dramatic, huge typography, film-title energy, premium atmosphere.
- **Colors:**
    - **Deep Black:** Backgrounds.
    - **Bone:** Primary text.
    - **Lime:** Micro-accents (status, active states).
    - **Ember:** Cinematic accents (highlights, key visuals).
    - **Titanium:** Metadata and secondary information.
- **Typography:**
    - **Space Grotesk:** Dominant (Headlines, huge titles).
    - **IBM Plex Mono:** Secondary (Metadata, labels, "director's notes").

## Structure & Pages
1. **Homepage:** Giant hero headline, RU/EN switch, chapter-style navigation.
2. **Proof Archive:** Dossiers as scenes/title cards. Visual story covers and cinematic scorecards.
3. **Dossier Detail:** One dossier per page. Huge titles, intense notes, titanium metadata panel.
4. **Verifier / About:** Credits-style layout for the author.
5. **Field Notes:** Director's log / notebook aesthetic.
6. **Scope:** Inquiry surface framed as a production brief.
7. **Links:** Concise, beautiful route hub.
