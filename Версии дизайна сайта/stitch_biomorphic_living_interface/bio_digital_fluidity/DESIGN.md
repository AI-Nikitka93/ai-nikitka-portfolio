## Brand & Style

This design system is a living interface that merges biological evolution with digital precision. It rejects the mechanical rigidity of industrial-era design in favor of organic growth and fluid movement. The brand personality is enigmatic, futuristic, and sentient—evoking the feeling of peering into a deep-sea hydrothermal vent where digital life forms are emerging.

The style is a hybrid of **Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Organicism**. It treats UI elements as cellular structures rather than boxes. Surrounding containers are not rigid rectangles; they are soft, asymmetrical membranes that pulsate with "living signal fields" represented by subtle glows and rhythmic blurs. The target experience is one of discovery and immersion, moving away from utility toward a symbiotic relationship between the user and the data.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the deep shadows of an organic abyss. The base color, an organic black-green, provides a dense, atmospheric foundation that absorbs light. 

- **Primary Base:** The deep black-green serves as the canvas, creating a sense of infinite depth.
- **Bone Text:** Provides high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure white, feeling weathered and natural.
- **Lime Signal Glow:** Used for primary interactions and active states, mimicking bioluminescent organisms.
- **Ember Warmth:** Reserved for critical alerts, high-energy data points, or "internal heat" within the interface.
- **Titanium Metadata:** A muted, metallic gray used for technical details, timestamps, and secondary information that sits behind the primary "living" content.

## Typography

Typography in this design system balances the futuristic geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the raw, technical precision of **IBM Plex Mono**. 

Headlines should feel large and impactful, often using tight letter spacing to create a sense of gravitational pull. Body text prioritizes readability within fluid containers. The use of IBM Plex Mono for labels and metadata introduces a "bio-sensor" aesthetic, as if the user is reading data logs generated by an organic processor. All text should be rendered with slightly softened contrast against the background to prevent visual fatigue in the dark environment.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system avoids rigid grids. It utilizes a **Fluid Layout Model** based on "Safe Areas" and "Centripetal Tension." Content is grouped into clusters that float within the black-green void, held together by proximity rather than alignment to vertical lines.

Instead of traditional columns, use concentric circles of influence and asymmetrical padding. Elements should appear to "settle" into place. Spacing is rhythmic and expansive, allowing the background to breathe. Margins are never uniform; use slightly larger padding on one side of a container to suggest organic growth or directional flow.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through **Atmospheric Layering** and **Luminous Diffusion**. There are no drop shadows in the traditional sense. Instead, higher-level elements use:

1.  **Backdrop Blurs:** High-intensity blurs (30px+) that pull the organic green background into the surface.
2.  **Internal Glows:** Subtle inner shadows using the Lime Signal color to suggest a thin, glowing membrane edge.
3.  **Variable Opacity:** Surfaces range from 40% to 80% opacity, allowing layers to overlap and create new color depths.
4.  **Signal Fields:** Elements closer to the user "vibrate" with a soft, outer bloom of Lime or Ember, suggesting they are energized and interactive.

## Shapes

The shape language is strictly **Non-Rectangular**. Surfaces should utilize extremely high border radii, often bordering on elliptical or "squircle" forms. 

Avoid 90-degree corners entirely. Use `rounded-xl` and `rounded-full` as the defaults for all interactive containers. For a truly experimental feel, use CSS `clip-path` or SVG masks to create slightly irregular, blob-like perimeters for larger sections. These "blobs" should feel like they are held by surface tension, ready to merge or split.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Large, pill-shaped triggers. The default state is a transparent "ghost" with a Lime border. Upon hover, the button should fill with a Lime-to-Green gradient and emit a soft glow (bloom).
- **Chips / Tags:** Small, semi-translucent capsules using IBM Plex Mono. These represent "data nodes" and should have a Titanium border.
- **Lists:** Not a vertical stack of lines, but a series of floating "cells." Each item is a soft-cornered pod with ample space between them.
- **Input Fields:** Minimalist under-glow lines rather than boxes. When focused, the line expands into a soft, glowing Lime aura that illuminates the Bone-colored text.
- **Cards:** Large, glassmorphic membranes with varying degrees of transparency. The top-left corner should always have a more pronounced radius than the bottom-right to imply an organic "head" and "tail."
- **Living Signals:** Pulse indicators (small glowing dots) that use the Ember color to indicate real-time data updates or "heartbeat" status of the system.
